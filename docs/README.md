# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management process documents used by OctoAcme. Together, they provide a simple, repeatable framework for turning ideas into delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a consistent project lifecycle:

- Initiation: validate the need, align stakeholders, and define success metrics.
- Planning: create the backlog, estimate scope, identify dependencies, and align milestones.
- Execution & Tracking: run standups, review progress, and escalate blockers when needed.
- Risk & Communication: monitor issues, share status, and escalate according to impact.
- Release & Deployment: verify quality, deploy with safeguards, and communicate outcomes.
- Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: capture lessons learned and turn them into action.

## Project Management Process Summary

- Initiation: confirm the business need, define stakeholders, and agree on measurable goals.
- Planning: turn the initiative into a clear backlog, estimate effort, and identify risks and dependencies.
- Execution: track progress, maintain team rhythm, and manage blockers with clear escalation paths.
- Risk & Communication: keep stakeholders informed, document issues, and escalate when impact grows.
- Release & Deployment: validate readiness, deploy safely, and announce outcomes.
- Retrospective: review what happened, capture action items, and improve the process over time.
